Our Goals

  • Empower Local Artists: Offer a platform for emerging and already established artsists to exhibit their work, gain recognitionn and build careers.
  • To Promote Art Education: To provide accessible workshops and lessons in visual arts to people interest in learning and developing their skills.
  • Cultivate Artistic Growth: To support the development of creative talents by offering, exposure and oppurtunities for collaboration in our organizations space
  • Building an art community: To create an art communtiy that allows art enthusiats, collectors and artists to discuss, celebrate and practice art.

Our Vision

Our Vision for KA Art Gallery is to become a social hub in the community,
where the arts are displayed and experienced.We aim to create a space where the only limit to your creativity is your imagination,
where artists of different backgrounds and skill levels can share their perspectives,
improve their skills and connect with wider audiences. Throught our efforts we hope to leave a positive impact on the cultural aspects of our community.

Our History

Established in 2024, by Kwazi Ayanda, the gallery and workshop began as a small space used to showcase artworks between friends, over time it grew into a space dedicated to uplifting local artists and fostering creativity.
Initially a one-room studio, the gallery quickly grew into a vibrant hub for monthly exhibitions and workshops.
Today KA Art Gallery has become a major part of Qgeberha's art scene, proving both emerging and established artists with a platform to connect and shocase their work within the community.

Our Mission

To serve as a creative space where local artists can thrive,
share their work and connect with the community by providing a welcoming environment for artistic education and expression,
we can make sure the next generation of artists never lacks inspiration.
