Welcome To The Gallery


Welcome to KA Art Gallery and workshop, a creative space that supports local talent for those who appreciate visual arts. We offer a platform for artists who’ve searched long and hard for a place to showcase their artworks and putting themselves on the map as a result. Our gallery acts as a hub for creativity, where aspiring and established artists can connect and support each other. Whether you’re an artist looking to have your work displayed for all to see, an enthusiast looking to find new artworks, or someone who’s genuinely passionate about practicing the craft. Our organization can support all your artistic endeavors. Join us in supporting a community where art is appreciated by everyone.
