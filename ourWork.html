OUR WORK

How We Operate



At KA Art Gallery, we're focused on uplifting local artistsa and muturing creativity in our community.
From hosting monthly exhibitions to offering hands-on workshops.
By providing a platform to showcase work and develop their skills,
We inspire and support the next generation of creative talent

What We Do

Workshops are hosted on weekends, to educate those who want to learn.
We host different types of lessons every weekend.
Live model classes, How to use watercolor, oil and acrylic paints,
how to market artworks on different platforms and expose them to established artists to learn from.

Our Activities

Hosting Exhibitions

Every month we use all the collection of artworks sent towards us and created in our own studio,
and showcase them to the public.This way art enthusiats can see the work of local artists,
giving them attention and helping them progress into becoming established artists in the community.
